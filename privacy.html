Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

This policy covers this website. It explains what happens to your information when you visit it. We've kept it short, because we collect very little.

What we collect

Nothing. This website has no sign-up, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. We don't track you and we don't build a profile of you.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you load a page, to keep the service secure and running. We don't receive or use this data. You can read how GitHub handles it in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

This website links to other pages, such as our brand picker and the App Store. Those pages have their own privacy policies. The brand picker runs in your browser, and what you enter there stays on your device until you choose to send it to us.

If you contact us

If you write to us, we use your message and contact details only to reply and to discuss working together. We don't share them with anyone else or use them for marketing.

Apps we build for creators

This policy doesn't cover the apps we build. Each app is published by its creator and comes with its own privacy policy, which you can find on its App Store page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Since this website collects none, there's usually nothing to request, but you're always welcome to ask.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
