Questions

If yours isn't here, just ask.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. The only thing you set up yourself is an Apple Developer account in your name ($99 a year, paid to Apple). After that, you choose your look and approve the App Store listing, and we handle everything else. We're here whenever you need help, before launch and after.

Why does the app go on my own Apple developer account?

Because then it's truly yours. The app appears on the App Store under your name and your brand, and the listing, the reviews, the subscribers and the money from Apple all go straight to you.

Do I have to write all the affirmations myself?

No. The app comes with hundreds of affirmations across 19 topics. You can add your own on top, and they appear in their own category with your name. But this is completely optional.

How long until my app is live?

It depends mostly on how quickly we get your picks, content and Apple account access, and on Apple's review, which we don't control. Usually it takes a month or two.

Can you guarantee downloads or income?

No one honestly can. How the app does depends on your audience and how you share it.

Is there an Android version?

Not at the moment. The app is made for iPhone. If your app does really well, we're happy to build an Android version for you too.

What about my followers' privacy?

The app shows no ads and doesn't track people. We prepare a privacy policy and terms of use in your name and host them for you.
