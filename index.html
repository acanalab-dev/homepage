Questions

If yours isn't here, just ask.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You pick your look in the brand picker, send us your affirmations and approve the App Store listing. We handle everything else.

Why does the app go on my own Apple developer account?

Apple requires apps built on a shared platform to be published by the owner of the content. It also means the App Store listing, the reviews and the money from Apple go straight to you.

Do I have to write all the affirmations myself?

No. The app comes with hundreds of affirmations across 19 topics. You can add your own on top, and they appear in their own category with your name.

How long until my app is live?

It depends mostly on how quickly we get your picks, content and Apple account access, and on Apple's review, which we don't control. We agree on a target launch date when we start.

Can you guarantee downloads or income?

No one honestly can. How the app does depends on your audience and how you share it. What we can promise is a polished app, a smooth launch and an app that keeps working.

What if I want to stop?

You can remove the app from sale at any time, or buy out our share with 60 days' notice for the greater of $10,000 or 24 times our average monthly share, and keep the app as it is.

Is there an Android version?

Not at the moment. The app is made for iPhone.

What about my followers' privacy?

The app shows no ads and doesn't track people. We prepare a privacy policy and terms of use in your name and host them for you.
